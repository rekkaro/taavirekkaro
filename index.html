exhibition text:

“do i need to say it out myself?”

taavi rekkaro

foku gallery

august-september 2026

A moment before i didn’t know what i was feeling, although my gut, my body already knew. Instead i had to listen to myself, which brought out those words with a strange sparkle. How to better understand my problems, those that need to live with me. Mistakes that gain strength or are hidden according to the phase of the moon, the sun, hunger, or when was the last time i had sex?

The photos and “objects” on display treat photography as something that should be felt, that has its own memory and where closeness becomes part of the experience. Together, the artworks create a dialogue between distance and intimacy. Moments in everyday life, asking how do we stay connected to what is important. How can we be sure you even were there - were those moments real? What have you lost to confusion or simply forgotten? Fleeting moments captured as photos, quotes, text, paintings, sculptures or who knows in what shape.

This series is an attempt to replace a crushing core belief and to find a way to find the part that’s actually important. On the gallery wall is a melting moment, consisting of works starting from 2023. Captured moments with good friends, from places full of emotions or just to feel alive or just a little bit good. To be present and to listen what the body/mind is looking to catch.

The exhibition wall where time could dissolve, but next to it where you can spend time. The wall doesn’t hold a single story, but you should have time to find your own narrative.

“Margus* waits and looks at me. I wait and look at him.

I ask: “Do i have to say it out myself?

Margus looks,
Margus widens his eyes and nods slightly.

I answer: “Because I am a bad person.””

conversation March 2021
*psychologist Margus

exhibition text:

In May of 2026, rasmus, tor and taavi went to visit the photo london art fair. the title of this show is from one of those mornings: taavi read out a memory from the previous night, where his good friend tor tried to get to the root of their cause.

“we like to be like sad jazz
we want to be like sad jazz”

without writing down, this or these moments would just be lived but forgotten. (it’s also fun to see tor reacting to his past thoughts.

for taavi, catching moments is sacred. how can you be sure you even were there - did these moments truly exist? what have you lost if they get mangled up or simply forgotten?

in his process taavi is trying to catch fleeting moments like these - whether they come in the shape of photos, quotes, texts, paintings, sculptures or pieces of tape. presented in vasli souza, båstad, is a similar dissolving snapshot of taavi’s ongoing project, consisting of photos and sculptures starting from 2023. captured moments of good friends, places full of emotions and just trying to feel alive or just a bit happy.
